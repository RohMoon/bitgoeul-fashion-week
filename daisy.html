DESIGNERS 2025

SOH

Daisy-Rose founded Sleight of Hand (SOH) on the cyclical concept of renewal through handmade culture. SOH refines sustainable practice in contemporary fashion, with each capsule reflecting the evolving connection between people, culture, and environment. Embracing dead-stock fabrics, circular methodologies, and traditional craftsmanship, SOH designs honor the human form and natural world, focusing on longevity. Through unique material, process, and collaboration, SOH creates a distinct visual language, instilling essential, timeless nature into each piece.
